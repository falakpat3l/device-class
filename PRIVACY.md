# Device Class Finder India privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Device Class Finder India is an Android app made by Falak Patel.

## In short

Device Class Finder India does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything stays on your phone.

## What the app keeps on your phone

- Nothing. Answers are only kept while the app is open.

This is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## No internet, no tracking

Device Class Finder India has no internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising ID. Links in the app (for example to the data sources) open in your browser.

## Permissions

- None. The app asks for no permissions at all.

## Children

The app is not made for children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
