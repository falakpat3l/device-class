## Store listing

App name (25/30):
Device Class Finder India

Short description (79/80):
Find the likely risk class (A to D) of a medical device under India's MDR 2017.

Full description (1062/4000):

Device Class Finder answers one question: which risk class is my medical device likely to be in, under India's Medical Device Rules, 2017?

Answer a few plain questions, one at a time, and see:
- The likely class: A, B, C or D
- The reason, in one line
- Which licensing authority handles it (State or Central, CDSCO)
- Every answer you gave, so you can check them

Handy for founders, regulatory and quality teams, students and pharmacists.

- "What the classes mean" explains classes A to D in one line each
- Copy result copies everything as plain text, ready for a note or email
- Back goes to the previous question at any time

Private by design
- Works fully offline
- No internet permission, no account, no ads, no tracking
- Your answers are not saved or sent anywhere

The questions follow the risk based rules in the First Schedule of the Medical Device Rules, 2017, which are based on the international GHTF model.

Guidance only, not regulatory advice. Always check the official rules and CDSCO's published classification lists.

Made by Falak Patel.
